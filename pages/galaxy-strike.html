Galaxy Strike

Unity 3D C# Cinemachine Unity VFX Unity Terrain

Project Overview

"Galaxy Strike" is an adrenaline-fueled sci-fi space combat game. Players jump into the cockpit to fight alongside Commander Nathanieal in a last-stand assault against overwhelming dark forces. The game focuses on high-speed dogfighting, requiring players to dodge terrain and enemy fire while blasting through enemy fighters to save the galaxy from total annihilation.

Technical Deep Dive & Game Logic

Building a flight combat game required a unique approach to physics, specifically overriding gravity and handling 6-degrees-of-freedom (6DOF) movement logic.

My Contributions

  • Flight Mechanics: Developed complex, physics-based flight mechanics and fluid player controls using C#, simulating momentum and thruster drag.
  • VFX & Environment: Utilized Unity Terrain to sculpt the planetary surface and integrated extensive laser and explosion particle systems via Unity VFX Graph.
  • Optimization: Implemented efficient memory management (Object Pooling) for the hundreds of laser projectiles fired simultaneously.

Game Mechanics & Physics

  • AI State Patterns: Engineered AI behaviors for both enemies and allied wingmen utilizing state patterns, allowing them to track targets, evade, and form up on the player.
  • Collision Detection: Optimized collision detection using primitive colliders (spheres and boxes) instead of mesh colliders to drastically improve performance during intense dogfights.
  • Cinemachine Integration: Used Cinemachine to create a dynamic chase camera that subtly shifts its target look-ahead distance based on player speed and pitch, enhancing the sensation of flight.

How It Was Built — Step by Step

01

Flight Model Prototyping

Developed a custom 6DOF (Six Degrees of Freedom) flight model in C#. Experimented with torque and thrust forces to create a satisfying, weighty spacecraft handling feel.

02

Environment & Skybox Setup

Designed a massive space environment with rotating planets and detailed nebulas. Configured a custom skybox and ambient lighting to create a deep, immersive galactic atmosphere.

03

Enemy AI Formation Logic

Programmed AI flight patterns using a combination of waypoint navigation and target tracking. Implemented formation behaviors for enemy squads to challenge the player.

04

Weaponry & VFX Integration

Developed a modular weapon system with laser fire and heat-seeking missiles. Integrated Unity's VFX Graph for high-performance laser trails and cinematic ship explosions.

05

Cinemachine Camera System

Set up dynamic Cinemachine cameras that adapt to the ship's movement. Added screen shake and field-of-view shifts to emphasize speed and impact during combat.

06

Game Flow & UI Polish

Finalized mission objectives, dialogue systems for Commander Nathanieal, and a futuristic cockpit HUD. Polished the overall experience with deep-space audio and UI transitions.

What I Accomplished

"Galaxy Strike" was a deep dive into non-standard player controllers. Moving away from standard bipedal characters to spacecraft required a solid understanding of Quaternions and local vs. global space transformations in Unity. I also successfully managed performance bottlenecks caused by heavy particle systems, learning how to batch particles and use GPU instancing.
