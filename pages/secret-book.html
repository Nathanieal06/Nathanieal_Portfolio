The Secret Book

Zimension Platform Web-based 3D No-Code

Project Overview

"The Secret Book" is a mystery-adventure web game built on the Zimension platform. You play as an archaeologist exploring a hidden cave, solving four unique, interconnected puzzles to uncover the legendary Secret Book and the ancient secrets of a lost civilization. This project secured 2nd Place at the Zimension Game Jam 2025 and was recognized by the Game Developer Association of India (GDAI).

Technical Deep Dive & Game Logic

Working within the constraints of a no-code web platform required creative problem solving to implement engaging game logic and atmospheric world-building.

My Contributions (Team Leader)

  • Game Design: Crafted the overall game narrative and designed a focused 5-minute prototype gameplay loop.
  • Map Layout: Hand-designed and optimized the 3D map layout to ensure performance on web browsers while maintaining visual fidelity.
  • Team Coordination: Led the team (collaborating with Mohit Pipaliya & Amit Shah), managing the timeline to deliver a winning prototype within the jam's constraints.

Game Mechanics

  • Puzzle Logic: Engineered 4 unique puzzles using the platform's visual scripting system, ensuring they scaled in difficulty and felt rewarding to solve.
  • Exploration Flow: Balanced exploration and puzzle flow to keep player engagement high without feeling lost in the environment.
  • Environmental Storytelling: Placed visual cues and utilized lighting to guide the player naturally through the cave system towards the Secret Book.

How It Was Built — Step by Step

Built on Zimension Platform — No-Code 3D Game Engine

⚡ 3-Day Game Jam (Zimension Game Jam 2025) — This entire game was designed, built, and submitted within just 3 days. No custom code was written; all game logic, interactions, and puzzle mechanics were created using Zimension's visual scripting and drag-and-drop toolset. The team of 3 (myself, Mohit Pipaliya & Amit Shah) finished in 2nd Place out of all participants.

01

Game Concept & Jam Kickoff

On Day 1, the team brainstormed the theme, settled on a mystery cave-archaeologist narrative, and assigned roles — I led as game designer and map builder while teammates handled asset sourcing.

02

Map Layout in Zimension

Used Zimension's 3D environment builder to design and lay out the cave interior — carving corridors, placing torches, archways, and treasure areas to guide the player naturally without a minimap.

03

Puzzle Design & Visual Scripting

Configured 4 unique puzzles using Zimension's node-based visual scripting — each with Boolean state variables and conditional triggers that unlock the next area only when solved correctly.

04

Lighting & Atmosphere

Set up dynamic lighting (torch flicker, ambient glow, shadow gradients) to create a moody, atmospheric cave environment that added emotional weight to the mystery narrative.

05

Playtesting & Final Polish

On Day 3, the entire game was playtested internally, puzzle difficulty was tuned, the narrative cinematic was added, and the project was published to Zimension's web platform before the deadline.

What I Accomplished

Leading a team to secure 2nd place in a competitive Game Jam taught me invaluable lessons in rapid prototyping, scoping, and effective communication. I learned how to maximize the potential of a proprietary engine (Zimension) and deliver a complete, polished experience under a strict deadline. Seeing our game featured on Zimension3d.com was a huge milestone.
