SIH Village Map

Unity 3D Level Design Environmental Art

Project Overview

Originally conceptualized and designed for a Smart India Hackathon project, this is a beautifully lit, immersive rural village map. The goal was to create a highly realistic, culturally authentic environment using strictly optimized free assets. The map serves as a showcase of my level design capabilities, focusing on mood, lighting, and spatial flow rather than complex code logic.

Technical Deep Dive & Level Design

Creating a believable world requires an eye for detail and an understanding of Unity's rendering pipelines to ensure it runs smoothly on target hardware.

My Contributions

  • Environmental Art: Focused strictly on Environmental Design and post-processing to create a warm, inviting rural aesthetic.
  • Terrain & Placement: Hand-placed terrain details, foliage, and structured the village grid to feel organic and lived-in.
  • Asset Curation: Sourced, optimized, and integrated a collection of free assets, ensuring their materials and textures were consistent.

Lighting & Optimization

  • Post-Processing: Utilized Unity's Post-Processing Stack to enhance color grading, ambient occlusion, and bloom, giving the map a cinematic look.
  • Baked Lighting: Configured baked Global Illumination (GI) to simulate realistic sunlight bouncing off dirt paths and rustic walls while maintaining high performance.
  • Occlusion Culling: Set up occlusion culling to ensure that objects outside the camera's view are not rendered, allowing the map to run efficiently even on lower-end machines.

How It Was Built — Step by Step

01

Asset Collection & Optimization

Sourced a variety of high-quality free assets suitable for a rural village theme. Optimized textures and mesh density to ensure they would perform well in a large-scale Unity scene.

02

Terrain Sculpting & Texturing

Used Unity's terrain tools to sculpt the village landscape, adding elevation, paths, and water features. Painted multi-layered textures to create realistic soil, grass, and rock surfaces.

03

Village Layout & Propping

Manually placed houses, fences, trees, and props to create an organic, lived-in feel. Focused on spatial flow and visual storytelling to make every corner of the village feel unique.

04

Lighting Setup & Baking

Configured a directional light for sunlight and used Unity's Lightmapper to bake global illumination. This pre-calculated light bounce significantly improved visual quality while maintaining performance.

05

Post-Processing Effects

Added a Post-Processing volume with color grading, bloom, and ambient occlusion. These effects unified the visual style and gave the map a cinematic, high-end look.

06

Final Performance Pass

Implemented occlusion culling and LOD (Level of Detail) groups for distant objects. Ran final performance tests to ensure stable frame rates across different hardware configurations.

What I Accomplished

This project was a deep dive into the art side of game development. I learned how crucial lighting and post-processing are to the overall "feel" of a game. By baking lightmaps and optimizing polygon counts on imported assets, I successfully balanced visual fidelity with performance, a critical skill for any level designer working in Unity.
